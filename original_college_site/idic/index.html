About the Conference

SJ-BIESH 2027 is being jointly organized by MBCCET and SJEC as part of their Silver Jubilee Celebrations, commemorating 25 years of excellence in education, research, and innovation. The conference aims to bring together researchers, academicians, industry professionals, and students from diverse disciplines to explore how intelligent technologies and interdisciplinary approaches can address contemporary challenges in engineering, science, and humanities. SJ-BIESH 2027 will serve as a vibrant platform for knowledge exchange, research collaboration, and promoting sustainable and impactful solutions for society.

About SJEC

St Joseph Engineering College (SJEC), Mangaluru – 575028

St Joseph Engineering College (SJEC), located at Vamanjoor in Mangaluru, Karnataka, is an autonomous institution established in 2002. The College is affiliated to Visvesvaraya Technological University (VTU), Belagavi, and recognised by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE), New Delhi. SJEC offers undergraduate, postgraduate and research programmes in Engineering, Business Administration and Computer Applications. The institution has been accredited with an A+ Grade by NAAC, and several of its programmes are accredited by the National Board of Accreditation (NBA).

Guided by its motto “Service and Excellence,” SJEC is committed to quality professional education through outcome-based learning, contemporary teaching practices and a strong emphasis on holistic development. The institution provides modern laboratories and academic facilities and promotes active engagement with industry, professional bodies and academic partners. Its learning environment encourages students to develop technical competence, leadership, creativity and a strong sense of professional and social responsibility.

Research, innovation and entrepreneurship form important components of SJEC's academic ecosystem. Faculty members and students are actively involved in research across Science, Technology and Management, supported by institutional research initiatives, externally funded projects, publications and collaborative activities. As SJEC marks its Silver Jubilee, the institution continues to strengthen its commitment to impactful research, innovation, interdisciplinary collaboration and engagement with society.

About MBCCET

Mar Baselios Christian College of Engineering and Technology (MBCCET), Kuttikkanam, Peermade – 685531

Mar Baselios Christian College of Engineering and Technology (MBCCET), Kuttikkanam, Peermade, Kerala, is a premier self-financing engineering institution established in 2001. Approved by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE), New Delhi, and affiliated with APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University (KTU), Kerala, the institution is managed by the Malankara Orthodox Syrian Church and is committed to imparting quality technical education rooted in academic excellence, professional ethics, and social responsibility.

MBCCET offers undergraduate engineering programs in several disciplines and provides a learner-centric environment supported by highly qualified faculty, modern laboratories, research initiatives, industry interaction, and innovation-driven learning. The institution strives to develop competent engineers who can address contemporary technological and societal challenges through creativity, critical thinking, and lifelong learning.

MBCCET is also recognized for its disciplined academic environment, value-based education, and strong emphasis on character building. The institution maintains a well-structured and student-friendly campus culture that promotes punctuality, professional conduct, ethical values, mutual respect, and academic commitment. The disciplined atmosphere, combined with excellent residential facilities and a vibrant student community, contributes significantly to students' holistic development.

With its blend of academic excellence, strict discipline, modern infrastructure, and location amidst the captivating landscapes of Kerala's Western Ghats, MBCCET offers an exceptional setting for education, research, innovation, and professional development. Guided by its vision of becoming an engineering institution of global standards, the college continues to make meaningful contributions to engineering education and technological advancement.

About Peermade, Kerala

Nestled in the serene hill station of Kuttikkanam in Idukki district, the campus is surrounded by lush tea plantations, spice gardens, rolling hills, and mist-covered valleys. The region enjoys a cool and pleasant climate for most of the year, making it an ideal destination for academic pursuits, conferences, and scholarly interactions. The refreshing mountain air, abundant greenery, and tranquil atmosphere provide an inspiring environment for learning, research, and innovation.

Peermade and its surrounding areas are among Kerala's most attractive tourist destinations. Delegates visiting MBCCET can explore renowned attractions such as Vagamon, known for its picturesque meadows and pine forests; Thekkady and Periyar Wildlife Sanctuary, famous for rich biodiversity and wildlife; Panchalimedu, a scenic hilltop viewpoint; and Parunthumpara, celebrated for its breathtaking panoramic views of the Western Ghats. The region's sprawling tea, coffee, and cardamom plantations add to its unique charm and natural beauty. The renowned tourist destination, Munnar, is also in close proximity.

About Mangaluru, Karnataka

Mangaluru, the headquarters of Dakshina Kannada district, is a vibrant coastal city situated along the Arabian Sea in Karnataka. Known as an important centre for education, commerce and culture, the city attracts students and professionals from across India and abroad. Mangaluru combines the character of a historic port city with a modern urban environment and is known for its beaches, places of worship, distinctive architecture, local markets and rich culinary traditions.

The city and the surrounding coastal region offer delegates an opportunity to experience the natural and cultural diversity of Karnataka's coast. Attractions in and around Mangaluru include scenic beaches, heritage and religious sites, lush landscapes and the distinctive traditions of coastal Karnataka. The region is also known for cultural forms such as Yakshagana and for its unique coastal cuisine. With the Arabian Sea to the west and the Western Ghats forming the broader eastern landscape of Dakshina Kannada, Mangaluru provides an attractive setting for academic interaction, networking and cultural exploration.
